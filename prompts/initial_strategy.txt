Use a continuous Worker-rush policy.

Every Base must continuously produce Workers whenever resources allow. Workers must harvest the nearest reachable resource and return carried resources to the nearest friendly Base so Worker production does not stop. Keep one Worker harvesting to fund the next Worker; every additional Worker should move toward and attack the enemy Base. Newly produced Workers follow the same harvest-then-attack cycle. If the enemy Base is not visible, move toward known enemy territory and attack the nearest enemy unit or structure encountered, returning to the enemy Base as the highest-priority target when it becomes visible. If enemies threaten a friendly Base, nearby Workers eliminate the immediate threat before resuming the rush. Do not build Barracks or produce non-Worker units.
