IMMUTABLE MICRORTS GAMEPLAY CONTRACT.

Treat this as a closed-world description of the game available to a strategy.
A policy may use any strategic identity, unit composition, timing, positioning,
branching, or opponent adaptation expressible with these mechanics. It may
depart substantially from its parent strategy. Do not assume an entity,
capability, observation, or action that is not listed here.

Game objective and world:
- Two players act on a rectangular grid measured in cells and game cycles.
- The evaluated game state is fully observable; there is no fog-of-war or
  information-revealing scouting mechanic.
- A player wins by eliminating every enemy-owned unit and building. A match
  that reaches its configured cycle limit without a winner is a draw.
- Cells may be blocked by terrain or occupied. Movement, production, and
  building placement require a legal reachable or adjacent cell as applicable.
- Resources are neutral map entities. Each player also has one scalar resource
  stockpile used to pay production and construction costs.

Complete entity set and production graph:
- Resource: neutral, stationary, harvestable, and not a player unit.
- Base: stationary stockpile; costs 10 resources, has 10 HP, and produces Worker.
- Barracks: stationary; costs 5 resources, has 4 HP, and produces Light, Heavy,
  or Ranged units.
- Worker: costs 1, has 1 HP, moves, attacks at range 1, harvests one resource at
  a time, returns carried resources to a friendly Base, and builds Base or
  Barracks.
- Light: costs 2, has 4 HP, moves fastest among the listed combat units, attacks
  at range 1, and deals 2 damage.
- Heavy: costs 2, has 4 HP, moves slower than Light, attacks at range 1, and
  deals 4 damage.
- Ranged: costs 2, has 1 HP, moves, attacks at range 3, and deals 1 damage.
- Base and Barracks do not move, harvest, or attack. Light, Heavy, and Ranged
  units do not harvest, return resources, construct buildings, or produce units.
- Default action durations in game cycles are: Worker move 10, attack 5,
  harvest 20, return 10; Light move 8 and attack 5; Heavy move 12 and attack 5;
  Ranged move 10 and attack 5; Base construction or Worker production 250;
  Barracks construction 200; Light production 80; Heavy production 120; Ranged
  production 100.

Complete strategic action set:
- Assign an action only to an idle friendly unit; one unit executes one action
  at a time and the action completes over game cycles.
- Move a movable unit through orthogonally adjacent legal cells. Competing
  movement or production into the same cell may be cancelled, so avoid
  conflicting destinations.
- Target an enemy-owned unit or building; move toward it when necessary and
  attack once it is within the attacker's range.
- Have a Worker harvest a Resource and return its carried resource to a
  friendly Base.
- Have a Base train a Worker into a free adjacent cell and pay its cost.
- Have a Barracks train Light, Heavy, or Ranged into a free adjacent cell and
  pay its cost.
- Have a Worker build a Base or Barracks on a legal free cell and pay its cost.
- Leave a unit idle when no higher-priority legal action applies.

Observable strategy state:
- Current game cycle, player side, player resource stockpile, and map width and
  height.
- Every current entity's type, owner, grid position, carried resource amount,
  current HP, and maximum HP.
- Whether a friendly unit is currently idle and whether a candidate grid cell
  is in bounds and free.
- Relative distance and counts may be derived from those observations.
- Opponent implementation name/class, hidden intent, future actions, wall-clock
  seconds, technology levels, upgrades, and any state not listed above are not
  observable strategy conditions. Translate opponent-specific evidence into
  responses to observable unit, building, economy, position, or timing cues.

Grounding requirement:
- Every condition in a generated policy must use observable state listed above.
- Every response and fallback must use the listed entities and legal actions.
- Before returning a policy or diagnosis, check every stated rule against this
  contract and reformulate any rule that cannot be executed in this closed world.
