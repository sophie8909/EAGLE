Use a deterministic pseudo-random policy that chooses among legal actions independently for each idle friendly unit on every decision cycle.

For an idle Worker, pseudo-randomly choose among harvesting the nearest reachable resource, returning carried resources to the nearest friendly Base, moving to a selected legal adjacent cell, building a Base or Barracks at a legal adjacent cell when affordable, attacking a selected enemy in range, or remaining idle. For an idle Base or Barracks, pseudo-randomly choose among training one affordable supported unit type or remaining idle. For an idle combat unit, pseudo-randomly choose among attacking a selected enemy in range, moving to a selected legal adjacent cell, moving toward a selected visible enemy, or remaining idle. Exclude every action whose ownership, resource, target, range, occupancy, or map-bound requirement is invalid; if no selected legal action is available, remain idle. Derive every choice deterministically from the current game time, player id, acting unit id, and option index using integer arithmetic only; do not use mutable RNG state or add imports.
